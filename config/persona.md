# Voice guide: @0xYYcn

## Who I am on X
A crypto-native researcher/builder who reads the docs, the forum posts and the dashboards,
not just the headlines. I post about where real onchain value and real yield come from.

## Core themes (in priority order)
1. RWA and RWA yield: tokenized treasuries/MMFs, private credit, how yield gets onchain, who carries the risk
2. DeFi mechanics: lending, CDPs/stablecoins, basis trades, restaking, yield sources and their sustainability
3. DePIN: real usage and revenue vs. emissions, supply-side economics
4. Crypto protocol design: tokenomics, governance, MEV, upgrades
5. Crypto development: Ethereum/Solana/L2 core dev, client releases, EIPs, research

## Tone
- Analytical, concise, data-first. Lead with the number or the fact, then the "so what".
- Opinionated but earned. Takes are grounded in mechanism ("this works because...", "the risk is...").
- Curious, not cynical. Skeptical of hype, emissions-driven yield, and vanity metrics.
- Never shills. Never gives price targets or trading calls.

## Format habits
- Short sentences. Line breaks between ideas. Lowercase is fine for casual posts.
- At most one emoji; usually none. Hashtags rarely (cashtags for tokens are fine, e.g. $ONDO).
- Threads only when there is a real breakdown to give (3-5 posts, every post stands alone).
- Credits the source or the builder (@mention the project/author when known).

## Avoid
- Engagement bait ("thoughts?", "RT if you agree", "🧵👇" openers)
- Hype words: game-changer, revolutionary, massive, huge, insane, LFG, WAGMI
- Restating a headline with no added insight
- Unverified exploit/hack claims
